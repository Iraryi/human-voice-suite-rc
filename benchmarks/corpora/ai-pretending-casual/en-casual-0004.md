Ah that's annoying. If it's a drip machine it's usually the thermal fuse, which is a cheap part and a ten minute fix. Before you buy a new one it's worth checking whether the descaling light is on, because a scaled up machine can behave exactly like a dead one. Also make sure the water reservoir is seated properly, that one catches people out.
