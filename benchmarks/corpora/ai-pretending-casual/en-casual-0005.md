Most people assume a phone battery wears out because of how many times you charge it. That's only half right. What actually wears a lithium battery down is heat, and time spent sitting at a full charge. Which is why leaving a laptop plugged in at 100 percent for a few weeks does more damage than a few hundred partial cycles. Worth knowing if you're trying to make a machine last.
