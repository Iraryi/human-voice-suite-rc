# Regional trade: what the agreement does

The agreement was signed last month. It covers regional trade between the neighbouring economies, and both sides describe it as the product of sustained dialogue rather than of one round of talks.

Industry observers have noted the deal's significance, and it has been cited in several major outlets. Experts quoted in that coverage say it could reshape supply chains across the region.

The partnership has continued through the challenges that followed the signing.

## Challenges and what comes next

Both sides say they remain committed to the framework and expect it to hold over the coming years, and they describe the agreement as an achievement for the region.
